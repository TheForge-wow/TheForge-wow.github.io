Salvage Station

One-click disenchanting for WoW Forever: see what every item gives, whether it's worth more sold, and clear your bags with one button.

Download on CurseForge See features View on GitHub

Features

One-button disenchanting

Every item you can disenchant in one list with its possible results: chance, average amount and range. Disenchant Next breaks the selected item and waits for the loot. Prefer something smaller? Switch to the compact bar.

Disenchant, vendor or auction?

A tooltip panel compares Disenchant, Vendor and Auction value side by side and gives a clear verdict. Items worth more sold get blue AH and gold Vendor tags.

Learns from you

Starts with WoW Forever disenchant data, including Forever-only changes. After 10 disenchants of the same kind it switches to your own results.

Protection & safety

An upgrade guard, item level and value protection, gear sets and an ignore list. Confirms before breaking Epics, Rare BoE items or valuable items.

Statistics

Session and all-time totals, materials received and their value, and your profit or loss compared with selling the items instead.

Auction house scanner

Finds gear on the auction house worth more disenchanted than its buyout. Nothing is ever bought without your click.

Also: TSM or Auctionator prices, a disenchant hotkey, six accent colors, a minimap button and data bar support. Every option has its own on/off switch.

How to install

CurseForge app

Easiest
  1. Open the CurseForge app and pick World of Warcraft.
  2. Choose your WoW Forever install.
  3. Search for Salvage Station and click Install.
  4. Launch the game. Updates install automatically.

Manual install

  1. Download the .zip from CurseForge.
  2. Extract the SalvageStation folder.
  3. Move it into Interface\AddOns in your WoW Forever folder.
  4. Restart the game and check it's enabled on the AddOns screen.

FAQ

Does it disenchant everything automatically?

No. The game needs a click or key press for every disenchant, so you press Disenchant Next (or your hotkey) for each item. Salvage Station does the finding, checking and aiming.

Will it break my gear?

Not if you don't want it to. The upgrade guard, item level and value protection, gear sets and ignore list keep items safe, and Epics, Rare BoE items and valuable items ask before they're broken.

Do I need TSM or Auctionator?

No. They're optional. If you have one, Salvage Station uses its prices for the auction values.

Does it work on Retail?

No, it's made for WoW Forever.

I found a bug. Where do I report it?

Type /salvage copy in game, copy the report, and paste it into The Forge Discord.
